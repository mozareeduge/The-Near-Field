# Taft Findings — Why v7 Changed

## 1. Test location

Taft, Yazd Province, Iran.

The test was useful because English-Wikipedia geographic density was sparse relative to Neyshabur.

---

# 2. Retrieval finding

Strict nearby coordinate-bearing Wikipedia material did not provide enough locally specific material.

However, relevant Wikipedia material existed elsewhere:
a Qanat article passage explicitly discussed Taft watermills in relation to qanat outflow and irrigation.

This exposed the flaw in:

```text
only nearby geotagged pages
→ Gatherer
```

Repair:

```text
nearby geotagged pages
→ if sparse
→ bounded exact-place enrichment
→ Gatherer
```

Enrichment remains separate from map geography.

---

# 3. Literary finding

An earlier generated Taft paragraph used:
- pump seals;
- black/grey variants;
- phone/customer/cousin coordination;
- troubleshooting.

The paragraph was concrete but felt:
- procedural;
- machinic;
- closed;
- geographically replaceable.

The prompt architecture had over-rewarded:
- object;
- money/time/task;
- repair/delivery;
- details that “do work.”

The model complied too efficiently.

---

# 4. Literary repair

### Removed/superseded pressure
`every detail must do two jobs`

### Current pressure
A detail may matter through:
- relation;
- habit;
- body;
- social position;
- time;
- materiality;
- discrepancy;
- rhythm;
- practical consequence.

### New primary rule
`enter a life already underway`

### New rejection
A closed problem→solution incident is weak when no wider life exceeds the task.

### New pseudo-specificity guard
A specialized technical/local prop needs a credible reason beyond “make it concrete.”

---

# 5. v7 Taft simulation

The v7 QA later simulated a family/visit situation involving:
- a mother;
- an aunt;
- repeated Thursday avoidance;
- Taft;
- Bagh-e Golestan / Sadri Garden.

The QA observed:
- stronger life continuity;
- no technical-prop machinery;
- no garden→paradise symbolism;
- no invented current conditions;
- residue ending.

It also explicitly kept **local necessity** at only 3/5 in that self-simulation.

So Taft remains a required regression fixture rather than a solved proof.

---

# 6. App consequence

Nearby Field must visibly distinguish:
- real geographic node;
- non-geographic local enrichment.

When Taft is sparse:
the map should not manufacture extra points.

The loader can say:
`field sparse`
→ `reading local traces`

The paragraph may use or ignore enrichment depending on the human situation; forcing it would recreate source-display writing.
