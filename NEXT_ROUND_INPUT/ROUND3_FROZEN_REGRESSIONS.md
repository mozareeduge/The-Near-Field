# Round 3 frozen regressions

These expectations cannot be weakened to make the final release pass.

## From Round 1

1. Public Nominatim is not a client-side autocomplete backend.
2. Coordinate presence alone does not make an English-Wikipedia page useful evidence.
3. Short administrative/census settlement stubs do not satisfy the three-useful-page threshold.
4. Logical field expansion is 1 km → 3 km → 10 km from one bounded 10 km geographic pool.
5. Geographic candidate cap is 16; per-candidate extract cap is 110 words.
6. Sparse at 10 km remains sparse; textual enrichment cannot manufacture geographic density.
7. Enrichment contains no coordinates and never becomes a map/movement node.
8. Participant-local date is preserved when supplied.
9. A source/code inspection is not a browser/runtime pass.

## From Round 2

10. Production literary architecture remains exactly two normal model roles: Gatherer then Synthesizer, with at most one bounded repair call per invalid stage.
11. Gatherer can select only known candidate IDs and must preserve exact source identity.
12. Gatherer may not leak plot/fiction into its output.
13. Raw GPS is not model material.
14. Synthesizer input cannot contain raw candidate-page extracts, unselected candidate material, route GeoJSON, or raw GPS.
15. One-paragraph structure and final character-offset bindings are validated before completion.
16. Enrichment evidence may inform local material but cannot acquire a coordinate through the model.
17. `VERIFIED` requires successful route-provider evidence for the exact ordered selected points **plus route geometry**. Matrix-only/documentary/coordinate evidence is insufficient.
18. Routing/provider failure preserves the selected field and downgrades honestly.
19. Unselected field candidates remain perceptible context after Gatherer selection.
20. Map, register, and prose refer to the same selected-place address for hover/focus/click/touch semantics.
21. Later-stage failure preserves earlier validated stages; retry is stage-local.
22. The old Harpers Ferry `VERIFIED` fixture is historical evidence, not current route authority.
23. Provisional R2 models cannot become final merely because the integration works.
24. Browser/live-provider gaps cannot be closed by mocks, source inspection, or the standalone fixture.
